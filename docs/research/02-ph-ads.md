# 02 — Product Hunt & Market Leaders: AI Video Ads / AI UGC Ads / Performance Creative (2024–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-13. Method: web search only. Direct page fetching was blocked, so every fact here comes from search-result summaries. Product Hunt (PH) launch pages could not be opened. Upvote counts and daily ranks are therefore sparse, and some come from third-party PH trackers (hunted.space, aitoolly). Prices are in USD unless stated. Each price carries the date of the source that reported it. Most price data comes from third-party review blogs, many of them written by competitors (Creatify, Zeely, Superscale, Sparkiz, GetHookd and others publish "X pricing" pages about rivals). **Check vendor pricing pages before quoting any number.** Items marked (unverified) appeared in only one weak source or conflict with other sources.

---

## 0. Executive summary (what matters for a Hebrew, niche-based "סרטוני תדמית" platform)

1. **The market has settled on a single core flow: "paste a URL, get N ad variants."** Creatify, Topview, Higgsfield (Click-to-Ad), HeyGen (URL-to-Video), Zeely, Tagshop, VidAU, Ava Studio, Arcads (AI Ad Agent) and AdGen AI all start from a product or website link. The tool scrapes images, prices and copy, writes scripts, casts an avatar, adds voiceover, captions and music, and exports in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9.
2. **2025–2026 shift: from "generator" to "agent."** Examples: Creatify AdMax and AI Media Buyer (Jun/Jul 2026), Arcads "Mark" with brand memory (Aug 2026), HeyGen Video Agent (PH #3, Jun 2025), Topview "Agent OS", Ava Studio ("AI creative team", PH #3, May 2026), Viva Labs ("Cursor for short-form video ads"), and TikTok Symphony Agent (Jun 2026). Agents research the brand and competitors, write angles and hooks, generate 10–50+ variants, and in some cases launch and optimize the campaigns.
3. **Hooks are the main lever, and the leaders all ship hook tooling:**
   - Zeely: "130+ smart hook formats" (vendor claim)
   - Higgsfield Marketing Studio: hook picklists inside presets
   - Mirage/Captions Ad Studio: hook, one-liner and CTA remixes
   - Creatify: script writer that outputs 5–10 variants with platform-specific hooks, plus an inspiration library and AdClone, which copies a competitor ad's *structure*
   - Quickads: "steal winning hooks" from a library of 30M+ ads
   - Icon: scripts based on winning ads
   - Stand-alone hook libraries: Hooked.so (752+ hooks in 24 categories), ContentHooks (hooks from videos with 1M+ views, searchable by niche), Hook iOS app, UGC Warehouse
   
   **None of these is Hebrew or Israel-specific, and none organizes hooks by local service niche** (dentist, lawyer, real-estate agent, restaurant). That is the opening.
4. **Hebrew is the clearest gap.**
   - Creatify's help center lists 29 languages, and Hebrew is not among them.
   - Arcads advertises "30+ languages", but sources say Hebrew is not listed.
   - Predis lists 18 languages, without Hebrew.
   - HeyGen *does* market Hebrew TTS, lip-synced translation and API narration.
   - The only Hebrew-native AI UGC ad tool found is **Sparkiz** (sparkiz.ai): Hebrew/English/Russian, a $3 first video, then $49/mo (self-reported).
5. **Typical pricing:**
   - Entry: $15–$59/mo (Higgsfield $15–19, Topview $16–29, Creatify $39, Zeely $29.95, MakeUGC $59, Predis $19–32).
   - Pro: ~$49–$149/mo.
   - "Agency" and premium UGC: $220–$999+/mo (Arcads $110–$550+, Mirage Ad Studio $799/mo, AdCreative Ultimate $999).
   - Effective cost per 30-second AI ad: **~$2–$11** (Creatify ~$3.30–3.90, Higgsfield ~$2–9, Arcads ~$11).
   - Human UGC benchmark: Billo ~$59–99 per video.
   - Credits are the universal billing unit, and reviewers' top complaints are confusing credits, expiring credits, $1 trials that cannot finish a video, and auto-renew billing.

### 1.1 Creatify (creatify.ai): category leader for URL-to-video ads
- **PH page:** "Creatify: The AI Video Ad Maker" — https://www.producthunt.com/products/creatify. PH description as quoted in a search summary: it turns "any product URL into high converting video ads", covering "product analysis, script, visuals, and voiceover". The page also calls itself "The #1 AI video ad maker", which is a self-description (https://www.producthunt.com/products/creatify).
- **PH launches:**
  - Creatify AI (original): Dec 19, 2023
  - Creatify Avatar: Aug 7, 2024
  - Creatify 2.0: Aug 29, 2024
  - Creatify Product Video: May 5, 2025
  - AI Media Buyer: Jul 13, 2026 on PH (Creatify's own blog dates the announcement Jun 18, 2026)
  
  Sources: https://www.producthunt.com/products/creatify/reviews, https://hunted.space/product/creatify-product-video, https://creatify.ai/blog/introducing-ai-media-buyer-the-ai-agent-that-runs-your-ads
- **PH rating:** 4.3/5 from 54 reviews. Praise: fast and easy. Complaints: confusing credit usage, expiring credits, limited scene-by-scene control, billing and auto-renew issues (https://www.producthunt.com/products/creatify).
- **PH rank:** Creatify's homepage claims "Product Hunt #1 Product of the Day" without saying which launch earned it (unverified; https://creatify.ai/). Upvote counts were not found.
- **Scale:**
  - $9M+ ARR within 18 months, $15.5M Series A (May 28, 2025) — https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250528506486/en/
  - A later job listing claims $10M+ ARR, 1.5M+ users, 10,000+ teams (https://builtin.com/job/growth-product-manager/7021466)
- **Workflow:**
  1. Paste a product URL.
  2. Creatify analyzes the product.
  3. It produces 5–10 script variants "with platform-specific hooks, CTAs, and benefit-driven copy."
  4. It adds an avatar, voice, B-roll and captions.
  5. It renders 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 versions.
  
  Sources: https://creatify.ai/features/url-to-video, https://creatify.ai/features/ai-video-generator

  - **AdClone:** analyzes a competitor ad's *structure* (hook style, pacing, CTA flow) and writes original scripts (https://creatify.ai/features/ad-clone).
  - **Inspiration / AI Ad Library:** "every template … comes from real ads that worked," broken down into replicable formats (https://creatify.ai/blog/creatify-ai---ai-ad-library-overview). One review claims AdMax draws on 10M+ Meta Ad Library ads, browsable by industry, style and platform (unverified; https://aiixx.ai/blog/creatify-ai-review-the-ad-agency-that-lives-inside-a-browser-tab).
  - **AdMax:** an "AI ad agent" with insights on hooks, formats, CTAs, lengths and fatigue (https://creatify.ai/admax).
  - **AI Media Buyer:** connects to ad accounts and runs them; its FAQ says it is powered by Claude Opus 4.7 (https://creatify.ai/blog/introducing-ai-media-buyer-the-ai-agent-that-runs-your-ads).
  - **Aurora:** proprietary image-to-video avatar/lip-sync model, also sold through fal.ai, Runware and ElevenLabs (https://creatify.ai/blog/aurora-image-to-video-ai-avatar-model-for-studio-grade-ads).
  - "14 types of AI shorts" (https://creatify.ai/creatify-2.0).
- **Avatars and voices:** 1,500+ avatars (Starter plan shows 300); 140+ voices; "75+ languages" in marketing (https://creatify.ai/features/ai-influencer-generator, https://creatify.ai/features/text-to-speech).
- **Hebrew:** **not confirmed.** The help center's supported-languages article lists 29 languages, without Hebrew (https://help.creatify.ai/en/articles/9348010-supported-languages).
- **Pricing** (multiple sources, Jun–Sep 2026; https://www.wireflow.ai/blog/creatify-pricing, https://shhots.ai/blog/creatify-pricing/):

  | Plan | Price | Credits | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Free | $0 | 10/mo | watermark, about 2 short ads |
  | Starter | $39/mo | 100 | 1 seat, 2-minute cap |
  | Pro | $99/mo | 300 (packs up to 5,000) | custom avatars, 5 seats, competitor ad tracking |
  | Enterprise | custom | — | white-label |

  - Credit rule: **5 credits per 15 s of video**. A 30-second ad costs about 10 credits, which is about $3.30–3.90.
  - Annual billing is "up to 50% off", but the reported annual figures conflict.
- **Target customer:** e-commerce/DTC, performance marketers, agencies.
- **UX to copy:** URL in → product card → several scripts with hook labels → avatar picker → batch render; inspiration gallery filtered by industry; "clone this ad's structure".

### 1.2 Arcads (arcads.ai): pure AI-actor UGC for direct response
- **PH:** a reviews page exists (https://www.producthunt.com/products/arcads/reviews). It says the product launched in 2024 and hit a demand spike that forced a temporary waitlist. The launch date, rank and upvotes were not found. Founders: Dylan Fournier and Romain Torres (https://app.dealroom.co/companies/arcads_ai).
- **Scale:** Latka reports $15M ARR and $16M raised (unverified; https://getlatka.com/companies/arcads.ai). A Digg summary dated Aug 4, 2026 reports a $16M raise to launch the agent "Mark" with brand memory (https://digg.com/tech/vhu9mk76).
- **Workflow:**
  1. Write or paste a script.
  2. Pick from 1,000+ AI actors.
  3. Generate talking-head UGC.
  4. Batch hooks × actors × tones.
- **AI Ad Agent / "Mark" (2026):** paste a website; it scrapes the site, researches the industry and audience, pulls visual assets, brainstorms angles, and generates static ads plus AI UGC videos (https://x.com/Just_sharon7/status/2086791111188865460). Mark "learns your brand, reverse-engineers your competitors' best-performing ads, and ships fully edited ads" (https://agent.arcads.ai/).
- **Hooks:** there is no dedicated hook library. Hooks are handled by rewriting the script's opening line and batch-generating hook × actor variants (https://www.therundown.ai/tools/arcads, https://youraifinder.com/tool/arcads).
- **Languages:** "30+ languages", but Hebrew is **not listed**, according to the Hebrew-focused competitor Sparkiz (treat with caution; https://sparkiz.ai/compare/arcads-vs-heygen/).
- **Pricing:** not public; the official pricing page returns 404. Third-party reports from Jun–Aug 2026:
  - Starter ~$110/mo for 10 videos
  - Creator ~$220/mo for 20 videos
  - Pro/top tier ~$385–$550/mo, with API access
  - About **$11 per video**, since short ads round up to a minute and a talking actor costs about 800 credits per minute
  - No free trial
  
  Sources: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/arcads-ai-pricing, https://www.wireflow.ai/blog/arcads-pricing
- **Target customer:** DTC, app and performance teams on Meta and TikTok.
- **Differentiators:** the largest actor library; realism.
- **UX to copy:** a "matrix" builder (N hooks × M actors = N·M videos).

## 5. Top 15 features and UX patterns a Hebrew niche-based platform should copy (b)

1. **One-field onboarding: "הדביקו קישור לאתר / לעמוד מוצר / לעמוד אינסטגרם."** Auto-extract logo, brand colors, images, prices and USPs into an editable brand card (Higgsfield Click-to-Ad, Creatify, Topview). Add a "no website? fill in manually" fallback (Zeely), which matters for Israeli service SMBs that often have no site.
2. **Niche picker first** (dentist, real estate, restaurant, lawyer, beauty salon, fitness, e-commerce…), which preloads templates, tone, hooks and CTA conventions. Zeely's 100+ industry templates are the closest precedent. No one does this for *service businesses* in Hebrew.
3. **A public, SEO-indexed Hebrew hook library by niche.** Each hook card shows the text, the hook type, an example video and a "צור סרטון עם ההוק הזה" button. Follows Hooked.so (752 hooks in 24 categories), ContentHooks (niche search) and Socialync (ranked by retention). Doubles as a lead magnet.
4. **A hook-type taxonomy with tone toggles:**
   - Question, statistic, problem/solution, bold claim, "stunt" vs "subtle", POV, testimonial (Mirage, Higgsfield)
   - Copy frameworks AIDA/PAS (Zeely)
   - Tag each generated hook with its type so users learn what works
5. **Variant matrix ("N hooks × M presenters × K CTAs").** One click produces 10–50 variants with clear credit cost before rendering (Creatify Batch Mode, Arcads, Ava Studio 50+). Show a grid preview and let users pick winners.
6. **AdClone / "clone a structure":** paste any ad link or upload a video; the AI extracts its structure (hook, pacing, CTA) and rebuilds it for your brand without copying content (Creatify AdClone, Icon).
7. **Inspiration gallery of "winning" formats** filterable by niche, platform (Reels/TikTok/YouTube Shorts/Facebook) and goal (leads, sales, branding). Each format is a reusable template (Creatify inspiration library, Icon's 18 formats, Higgsfield presets).
8. **Preset-based video styles with visual thumbnails:** UGC testimonial, tutorial, unboxing, product review, TV spot, before/after, "a day at the business", talking-head owner, b-roll plus voiceover (Higgsfield Marketing Studio, UGC Factory 40+ templates).
9. **Hebrew-first presenters and voices:** native Hebrew TTS (male/female, ages, accents), accurate RTL captions with burned-in animated subtitles, and optional lip-synced avatars. This is the main moat, since Creatify, Arcads and Predis do not list Hebrew (HeyGen and Sparkiz do).
10. **"Product/service in hand":** the avatar holds or demos the product (MakeUGC, Topview, Tagshop). For services, the equivalent is the avatar in the business setting, e.g. a clinic or office background generated from uploaded photos.
11. **A script editor next to a scene timeline, with per-scene regenerate.** Users complain about Creatify's lack of scene-by-scene control. Add a CapCut-like light editor (Icon) for text, music, scene swap and logo end-card.
12. **Multi-format export in one click:** 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9 with safe zones; direct publish or schedule to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok (VIDEO AI ME publishes to 15 platforms; Zeely launches to Meta).
13. **Predictive "creative score" and hook-strength meter** before render (AdCreative.ai Creative Scoring, Pencil), with post-launch hook-rate, CTR and ROAS feedback (Billo analytics, Creatify AdMax, AI Media Buyer) feeding back into hook rankings.
14. **An agent mode ("סוכן קריאייטיב"):** chat brief → research → angles → scripts → videos (Ava Studio, Viva Labs, Arcads Mark, HeyGen Video Agent, Topview Agent OS, TikTok Symphony Agent). Keep *brand memory* across sessions (Arcads Mark, Meta Brand Memory).
15. **Transparent, user-friendly credits:**
    - Show cost per video *before* generating
    - Rollover credits
    - Cheap first video (Sparkiz $3, MakeUGC/Quickads $1, but make sure the trial can actually finish a full video)
    - Easy cancel in-app
    - Clear output ownership
    
    These answer the most common complaints in PH and Trustpilot reviews: expiring or confusing credits (Creatify), $1 trials that fail (MakeUGC), weekly-price surprises (VidAU), charges before the trial ends (Quickads), and Pencil keeping output ownership.

Bonus patterns:
- SEO "X vs Y" and "X pricing" pages in Hebrew (Superscale, Sparkiz, Creatify all do this at scale)
- A white-label/agency tier (Creatify Enterprise)
- Done-for-you hybrid packages (Icon's pivot to human-filmed UGC)
