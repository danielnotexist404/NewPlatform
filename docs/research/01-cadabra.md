# 01: Cadabra AI (קאדאברה) at livinginmovie.com: competitor deep-dive

*Research date: 2026-10-13. Method: about 40 WebSearch queries in Hebrew and English. Direct page fetching was blocked, so every fact here comes from search-engine snippets and summaries of the indexed pages. One search noted that the livinginmovie.com homepage snapshot was "indexed about 111 days ago", so it dates from about late June 2026. Prices and engine lists may have changed since then.*

**Confidence legend:** (vendor claim) means the competitor says it about itself. (unverified) means one weak or ambiguous source. (inference) means my own reasoning, not a fact.

---

## 0. TL;DR

- **What it is:** Cadabra AI / קאדאברה is an Israeli, Hebrew-first **multi-model AI aggregator**, not a business-video tool. Its tagline is "פלטפורמה ליצירת סרטים בעברית", and it runs on the domain **livinginmovie.com**. It puts **30+ engines** (video, image, voice, music, talking avatars, lip-sync, 3D, subtitles, editing, 4K upscale) behind one Hebrew UI and **one credit balance**. Source: https://www.livinginmovie.com/
- **Monetization (vendor claim):** one-time **credit packs in USD** with **no monthly subscription**: Basic $19 / 105 credits, Pro $39 / 220, Ultra $69 / 420, Team $179 / 1,200. Failed generations refund credits automatically, and "one credit works on every engine". Source: https://www.livinginmovie.com/
- **Second business line:** a Hebrew **AI video/image/avatar course**, billed as "הקורס הגדול בישראל". It covers Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.0 and Hailuo, is in Hebrew with personal guidance, and needs no technical background. I found **no price or syllabus**. The founder's project page also mentions a digital community, lectures, workshops and online training. Sources: https://www.livinginmovie.com/ and https://missfixtheuniverse.com/mf-project/17826/
- **Founder:** the Miss Fix the Universe page (a women-entrepreneurs grant program run with Bank Hapoalim and the Israel Women's Network) describes a **former ICU nurse**. Her mission is to make AI accessible to everyone, "especially women and communities in Israel". One search summary named her **Meital Sela (מיטל סלע)**, but that is **(unverified)**: no other source ties the name to Cadabra. Source: https://missfixtheuniverse.com/mf-project/17826/
- **No sign of niche templates, business promo-video flows or hook libraries.** I found no independent press, reviews, Trustpilot page or social proof for this Cadabra. The brand name collides with at least six other "Cadabra" products, which hurts its SEO.
- **Opportunity:** Cadabra is a *tool box for hobbyists and learners*. A competitor that sells *finished, niche-specific business outcomes* (promo videos, reels with proven Hebrew hooks, brand kits, ILS pricing, an SMB-focused UX) would occupy a different and more monetizable position. The closest Israeli SMB players are **Sparkiz** (AI UGC ads in Hebrew), **FARO** (done-for-you AI-model ads, ₪490 to ₪2,990) and **Kossem** (branded carousels and video for SMBs).

## 3. Pricing (vendor claim, homepage snapshot about June/July 2026)

Model: **credits, not a subscription**. "תשלום לפי יצירה, בלי מנוי חודשי"; "אין חיוב חודשי אוטומטי"; "יצירה שנכשלת טכנית חוזרת ליתרה אוטומטית"; "קרדיט אחד עובד על כל מנוע במערכת"; "Each engine consumes a different number of credits according to the price list." The structured data lists a price range of **$19 to $179 (4 offers)**. Source: https://www.livinginmovie.com/

| Plan | Label (vendor) | Credits | Price (USD) | $ per credit | Approx. ILS* |

\*The ILS figures are my conversion at an assumed 3.6 to 3.75 ILS/USD. They are **not** prices Cadabra publishes. **Cadabra prices in USD, not ILS**, which is a friction point for Israeli SMBs who expect ₪ prices with a VAT invoice.

- The volume discount is shallow: about 18% cheaper per credit at Team versus Basic.
- **The per-engine credit cost table was not retrievable.** Rough math: Kling 3.0 or Seedance 2.0 cost about $0.07 to $0.30 per second at wholesale (fal.ai and others; not verified here). At $0.15 to $0.18 per credit, a 105-credit Basic pack probably covers only a handful of 5 to 10 second premium clips (inference).
- **Free trial / signup credits:** **not found.** The Basic $19 pack is framed as the "first taste". Source: https://www.livinginmovie.com/
- **Credit expiry:** not stated in the snippets.

## 9. SWOT: Cadabra AI

**Strengths**
- First-mover **Hebrew-first multi-engine aggregator** with Hebrew UI, subtitles and narration.
- **No-subscription credit packs** with an **automatic refund on failed generations** and **one balance across all engines**. These are trust-building, low-friction mechanics.
- Broad capability surface: 30+ engines, 4K, talking avatars, lip-sync, music, 3D.
- **Education funnel** (course, workshops, lectures, community) that creates a captive user base and teaches users to consume credits.
- A mission-driven founder story (ICU nurse, empowering women and the periphery) with Miss Fix the Universe recognition. That works well for PR, grants and B2G or community partnerships.

**Weaknesses**
- **Tool box, not outcome:** users must know which engine to pick, how to prompt, and how to edit. There are no visible niche templates, scripts, hooks or brand kits.
- **USD pricing** for an Israeli audience, with no ILS or VAT-invoice framing visible. A **$19 entry** with **no free trial found** is a barrier for SMBs.
- Shallow volume discount (about 18% at Team) and an opaque per-engine credit table.
- **Very weak discoverability:** a confusing domain (livinginmovie) versus brand (Cadabra), heavy name collisions, no press, no third-party reviews, and an unclear social presence (the YouTube link points to "@12thFloorDigital").
- Likely a small team, which carries a risk of slow engine updates and support bottlenecks (inference).

**Opportunities**
- Move into **SMB business video** (promo videos, reels, ads) and **niche verticals**.
- Hebrew SEO is open for **business** intent keywords such as "סרטון תדמית AI", "סרטון לעסק", "הוקים לרילס" and "רעיונות לסרטונים ל[נישה]".
- B2B and organizational training using the Team tier and workshops.

**Threats**
- Well-funded global aggregators (Higgsfield, Freepik, Kolbo with 100+ models, VEED, CapCut/Pippit) are adding Hebrew.
- Model makers (Google Gemini/Veo, OpenAI) sell directly to consumers. Engine churn is also a risk (e.g. the reported Sora shutdown, unverified).
- Israeli SMB-focused entrants (**Sparkiz, FARO, Kossem, NitAi, Nivra**) are attacking the business segment from the "outcome" side.
- Thin margins on resale credits when wholesale model prices change.

## 10. Ten concrete gaps and opportunities for a niche-based business-promo + hooks competitor

1. **Outcome-first wizard instead of an engine picker.** "בחר נישה → מטרה (תדמית / רילס / מבצע / המלצה) → הזן פרטי עסק → קבל סרטון מוכן". Engines are auto-routed by the backend, so the user never sees "Kling vs Seedance". Cadabra exposes 30+ engines and no guided flow.
2. **Niche library with SEO pages.** Ship 30 to 60 Israeli verticals (מספרה, קוסמטיקאית, נדל"ן, עורך דין, מסעדה, מאמן כושר, קבלן שיפוצים, מרפאת שיניים, חנות אונליין, צימר, בית קפה, ביטוח, יועץ משכנתאות, גן אירועים, etc.). Each gets template scripts, shot lists, music mood, CTA and its own public Hebrew landing page, which captures "סרטון תדמית ל[נישה]" searches that Cadabra does not target.
3. **Public Hebrew hooks library per niche.** 50 to 200 tested opening lines per niche ("3 טעויות ש...", "אל תזמינו X לפני שאתם יודעים..."), tagged by emotion, format (POV, before/after, myth-busting) and platform (Reels, TikTok, Shorts). Make it free to browse for SEO and lead gen, with one-click "generate video from this hook". No evidence of this at Cadabra.
4. **ILS pricing with a VAT invoice, a real free trial, and SMB-friendly plans.** Show prices in ₪, issue a Hebrew tax invoice (חשבונית מס), let users pay with Bit, PayBox or an Israeli card processor, and give 1 to 2 free watermarked videos at signup. Cadabra charges in USD with no trial found. Keep Cadabra's good mechanics: credits that don't expire plus an auto-refund on failure.
5. **Brand kit and consistency.** Upload a logo, colors, fonts and business photos once, and apply them automatically to every video (intro/outro, lower thirds, Hebrew subtitles in brand style). Add consistent "פרזנטור / דמות קבועה" per business, which FARO sells as a premium (₪2,990/mo).
6. **Hebrew-perfect text layer.** Correct RTL subtitles, burned-in captions with niche styling, nikud-aware TTS pronunciation fixes, and on-screen Hebrew typography that AI video models still garble. Do it with deterministic rendering (e.g. Remotion / FFmpeg) over AI footage, not inside the model.
7. **Script and storyboard by an LLM (OpenRouter) from a business profile.** Auto-write a 15, 30 or 60 second script with hook, problem, solution, proof and CTA, then a shot list, then route each shot to a video model, then add VO (ElevenLabs or other) and music, then auto-edit. This is "the video editor replacement" and is a step beyond Cadabra's manual multi-tool approach.
8. **Multi-format, multi-variant output.** One brief should produce 9:16 Reels, 1:1 feed, 16:9 YouTube/website and 3 to 5 hook variants for A/B testing. Add a scheduler or export pack (captions, hashtags, thumbnail). This suits marketers and agencies, and no evidence shows Cadabra does it.
9. **Agency / white-label and team seats.** A dashboard for social-media managers who serve 10 to 50 SMB clients, with client workspaces, approval links, and per-client brand kits. Cadabra's "Team" plan is only a bigger credit pack.
10. **Trust and visibility.** A clear brand and domain (avoid the Cadabra/livinginmovie split), a public gallery of before/after examples per niche, real testimonials, case studies with ₪ savings ("חסכתם ₪X על עורך וידאו"), an AI-content labeling helper (FARO stresses platform labeling rules), and a commercial-use / licensing FAQ. Cadabra has no press or third-party reviews.

*Bonus quick wins:* a referral program paying in credits (Kolbo does this at 1.25×: https://kolbo.ai/changelog); a weekly "הוק השבוע" WhatsApp or Telegram channel; and Hebrew micro-courses bundled with credits (copying Cadabra's education funnel but SMB-focused).
