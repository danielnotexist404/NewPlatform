([jett.co.il](https://www.jett.co.il/social-media-management-price/), [kanmedia.co.il](https://kanmedia.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9/))
- Campaign-management retainers are commonly ₪1,500–6,000/month. Suggested starting daily budget is about ₪20–50. ([bidernet.co.il 2025 guide](https://bidernet.co.il/%d7%9e%d7%97%d7%99%d7%a8-%d7%a4%d7%a8%d7%a1%d7%95%d7%9d-%d7%91%d7%a4%d7%99%d7%99%d7%a1%d7%91%d7%95%d7%a7-%d7%9e%d7%93%d7%a8%d7%99%d7%9a-%d7%9c%d7%a9%d7%a0%d7%aa-2025/))
- Ad budget is paid to the platform directly, on top of management fees. Small businesses should plan for about ₪500–1,000/month per platform. ([calliber.co.il](https://calliber.co.il/prices-list/social-media-sponsored-campaigns/), [lemel.co.il](https://lemel.co.il/social-media-management-price/))

## Ideas worth taking as inspiration (not copying)
1. **WhatsApp as the control channel.** We already have approval links; add an opt-in WhatsApp approval and "your campaign is live / here are this week's results" summary (WhatsApp Business Platform, with templates approved by Meta).
2. **"Done for you" feeling for non-marketers**: the customer approves, the system does the rest.
3. **One monthly price that is clearly below an agency**: they start at ₪499; our plans are ₪119 / ₪289 / ₪990 incl. VAT, and our differentiator is that we also *create the video and launch/manage the paid campaign* inside one Hebrew interface.

## Updated positioning (founder's brief, 2026-10-17)
- **Who:** small businesses at the start of their way, and growing businesses, who want paid ads on Facebook/Instagram **without breaking the bank, without paying marketers thousands of shekels a month, and without understanding Ads Manager**.
- **Promise:** a button → a ready video → launched as a campaign from our interface → simple Hebrew results (spend, how many people saw it, how many messages/leads, cost per lead, which hook worked) → change budget / pause / boost the winner from the same place.
- **Price claim:** cheaper than an agency or a social-media manager (₪1,500–6,000/month in the sources above) and cheaper than AI managed-social services like the reference (from ₪499/month), while the ad budget goes directly from the customer's card to Meta. Any "cheaper than" claim on the site must cite the comparison and its date (consumer law, see Part 02 / App E "truth first").
- **Content engine:** lots of viral, trending formats ("trend templates"), refreshed weekly by the team, that any niche can produce in one click.
